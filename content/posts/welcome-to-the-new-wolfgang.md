For years, the story of Wolfgang e-Sports has been told one Facebook post at a time: a championship poster here, a roster announcement there. Over the years, those posts added up to nearly 30 championships. This site brings that record together in one place.

## What you'll find here

- **Merch**: the official Wolfgang collection, coming soon.
- **Achievements**: our full trophy cabinet, searchable by game and placement. Results reported by Gamer.LK or the Daily FT are marked as verified.
- **Blog**: stories, interviews and looks back at the moments that built the pack.

> Fight like a wolf.

## What's next?

We'd love to hear from players, creators and partners who want to help write Wolfgang's next chapter. Head to the [contact page](/contact) and tell us about yourself.
