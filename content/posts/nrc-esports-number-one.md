Few rosters have dominated a Sri Lankan esports scene the way WG NRC Esports dominated PUBG Mobile. By the end of the 2020/21 season, the team was ranked the **No.1 PUBG Mobile team in Sri Lanka**, with 12 championships, two runner-up finishes and three third places to its name.

## The titles

NRC won almost every tournament it entered during that stretch. Among the highlights:

- **Red Bull M.E.O Season 4, Sri Lanka**: two chicken dinners and 30 kills in the Grand Finals, and a ticket to the Red Bull Asian Finals.
- **Dialog Axita Game Hero Season 3**: national champions, with a US$65,000 prize pool.
- **National Esports Championship**: second after day one, first by the final day.
- **TBS PUBG Mobile Series '21**: 57 kills and two chicken dinners across six matches.
- **Codashop Global Series Sri Lanka**, **TEPA Invitational**, **PMDS Series Season 4** and the **Ultimate PUBG Tournament** by BAE Squad.
