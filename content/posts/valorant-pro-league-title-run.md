When Gamer.LK launched the **Logitech G Valorant Pro League** on 16 December 2021, it invited Sri Lanka's top eight Valorant clans. Wolfgang was one of them. A month later, Wolfgang became the league's first-ever champions.

## The format

Teams were randomly seeded into a round-robin group stage, followed by knockout playoffs. Wolfgang came out of Group B and into the semi-finals.

## The run

- **Semi-final**: Wolfgang beat Noob Alliance.
- **Grand final**: Wolfgang beat Union Gaming to lift the trophy.

The result was reported in the Daily FT on 10 January 2022.

## The champions

- Lahiru "LiNo" Sandaruwan
- Shazlim "SCR1M" Amith
- Thulith "1KAPUMBA" De Zoysa
- Vibuda "Chromnz" De Zoysa
- Sithija "Gensh1" Amarasinghe
- Nisitha "Dopie" Weerasinghe
- Christopher Amarasinghe, team manager

## Part of a bigger story

The Pro League title capped a strong period for Wolfgang Valorant. Wolfgang Gunslingers won the Valorant event at the Dell Singer Online Esports Showdown 2021, and Wolfgang Mist'Walkers went 7–0 to win Division B of the Logitech G Play 2 Win Series Season 3. Mist'Walkers also reached the final of MSI Dome of Dragons.
